Follow your applications in your inbox

JobToFill reads the emails employers send you (“Thank you for applying”, “complete your online assessment”, “we’d like to invite you to interview”, “Unfortunately…”) and moves each application on by itself: applied → test → interview → offer. You then see where you’re being cut. It reads your inbox through Nylas, the connection you set up for codes; it never sends, moves or deletes an email, and nothing goes to the AI.

Connect your inbox first: Settings › Email codes.
