    How to read it
    f1 b1 i1
    A field, a button and an embedded frame, numbered down the page. Click a line to see it on the page.
    text*
    The kind of field (text, select, radio, file…). A star means the form says it’s required.
    → name.first ✓
    What JobToFill takes the field for, and your profile has the answer.
    ✗ no value
    JobToFill knows the question, but your profile has no answer for it yet.
    (none: for AI)
    No rule knows this question: a fill leaves it to the AI (with your API key) or to you.
    fill=filled
    What the last fill did with it: filled, nothing to put (no answer), skipped (it already had a value)…
    filled (answer withheld)
    It has an answer, but which one isn’t shown: equal-opportunity questions, pronouns, title and date of birth.
    INVALID
    The page says something is wrong with it, with the page’s own message.
    (left for you) (upload) (secret)
    A box JobToFill leaves for you to tick, an upload, a password or a card number.
